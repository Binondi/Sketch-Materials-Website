Who We Are

About Sketch Materials

Sketch Materials is a dedicated Android application designed to empower Sketchware Pro developers with a comprehensive and ever-growing toolkit. Our mission is simple: to make mobile app development more accessible, efficient, and enjoyable for everyone — from beginners taking their first steps in coding to experienced developers looking to speed up their workflow.

We provide hundreds of ready-to-use Java codes, custom blocks, local libraries, and Lottie animations — all curated and organized so you can find exactly what you need and integrate it into your Sketchware Pro projects with just a few taps.

How It All Began

Our Story

Sketch Materials was born out of a deep passion for mobile development and the Sketchware Pro community. As a developer working within Sketchware Pro, our founder — Binondi Borthakur — experienced firsthand the challenges of finding reliable code snippets, custom blocks, and resources scattered across forums, groups, and websites.

The idea was straightforward: what if all these essential resources were available in one single, beautifully organized app? That spark of inspiration led to the creation of Sketch Materials — a one-stop solution that has since grown into a thriving toolkit trusted by thousands of Sketchware Pro developers worldwide.

From a small personal project to a comprehensive resource hub, Sketch Materials continues to evolve with every update, driven by community feedback and the goal of making development accessible to all.

Our Resources

What We Offer

Java Codes

Access a massive library of ready-to-use Java code snippets covering UI components, animations, API integrations, file handling, and much more. Simply copy, paste, and build.

Custom Blocks

Extend Sketchware Pro's capabilities with our curated collection of custom blocks. From advanced logic to UI manipulation, these blocks simplify complex operations into drag-and-drop simplicity.

Local Libraries

Download and integrate powerful local libraries directly into your projects. Our collection includes libraries for networking, image loading, material design, and more.

Lottie Animations

Bring your apps to life with stunning Lottie animations. Browse our growing collection of lightweight, scalable animations perfect for loading screens, onboarding, and UI feedback.

Looking Ahead

Our Vision

"Making app development accessible to everyone, empowering the next generation of developers."

We believe that everyone should have the opportunity to create amazing mobile applications, regardless of their background or experience level. Sketchware Pro has already broken down barriers by enabling visual app development — and Sketch Materials takes it a step further by putting professional-grade resources right at your fingertips.

Our vision is to continuously grow our resource library, foster a supportive community, and inspire the next generation of developers to turn their ideas into reality. Every code snippet, every custom block, and every animation in Sketch Materials is a building block toward that future.
